What is Operators?
    The operation b/w multiple operands.

    A+B=AB
    Here A & B is operand , & + is Operator

Types
    1. Arthemetic : + , - , * , / , % , **
    2. Assignment : Value assigning , from right to left.
    3. Relational : Find relation b/w two or more operands.
    <,>,<=,>=,==,!=

    4. Logical : and , or , not
    Here we operate on multiple conditions.

    5. Membership : in , not in
    6. Indentify Operator : is , is not
